# Data Quality Report - Omnichannel Marketing Attribution

Generated by `Python/01_data_audit.py` from the raw CSVs in `Data/raw`. The dataset is **synthetic** and must never be presented as real client data.

**4 ERROR, 8 ANOMALY, 5 EXCEPTION, 3 INFO.** As-of date: **2026-08-31**.

| Class | Finding | Measured | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| ERROR | Calendar ends before the last fact date | dim_date ends 2026-08-31; opportunities run to 2026-10-09 and revenue to 2026-10-21. 244 opportunity and 209 revenue rows have no calendar day. | DimDate regenerated to 2027-06-30 (end of FY27) and checked against the supplied calendar for every overlapping day. |
| ERROR | TouchSequence contradicts TouchDate | For 35,409 of 50,000 leads (71%) a later sequence number carries an earlier date. Each touch type holds 19.9%-20.2% of first positions, so the sequence has no behavioural signal. | JourneyPosition orders by TouchDate, tie-break TouchSequence. Time-decay is defined in days and needs a real timeline. |
| ERROR | Funnel stage labels are stale | 2,428 leads with booked revenue are labelled 'Opportunity'. The label counts 4,131 customers; revenue shows 6,559. | DerivedStage from evidence: revenue -> Customer, opportunity -> Opportunity, otherwise the CRM label. |
| ERROR | Four currencies, no exchange rates | Spend arrives in AUD, EUR, GBP, USD with no FX table; revenue carries no currency column. | dbo.FxRate holds labelled static PLANNING rates to USD (not market data); revenue treated as USD. |
| ANOMALY | Revenue dated after the as-of date | Marketing activity stops at 2026-08-31, yet 244 opportunities and 209 revenue rows (888,614.96, 2.8% of revenue) are dated later. | Loaded and flagged IsAfterAsOf. EVERY figure anchors to the as-of date - the funnel included: an opportunity counts once opened and a customer once revenue is booked, by the as-of date. Post-period records appear only as data-quality figures. |
| ANOMALY | A lead's own campaign is unrelated to its journey | leads.CampaignID matches the first touch 0.3% and the last touch 0.3% of the time. | Attribution uses touchpoints only. The lead campaign is reported as 'lead source' for cost per lead. |
| ANOMALY | Touch type is independent of channel | Every channel carries all 5 touch types in near-equal shares (19.4%-20.6%) - for example 5,219 'Email Open' touches on Paid Search. | Touch type is not analysed by channel; journeys are analysed by position and channel only. |
| ANOMALY | Billing currency is unrelated to campaign region | In every region the most common currency covers only 25%-26% of spend rows. | Converted row by row on each row's own currency; no region-based currency is assumed. |
| ANOMALY | Ad data and CRM data are on different scales | The ad data records 254,585,358 clicks; the CRM holds 50,000 leads - one lead per 5,091 clicks (0.0196%), against the 2-5% typical of B2B landing pages. The ad side is internally consistent - clicks, impressions and spend move together (CTR 4.229%) - so the CRM extract covers a small fraction of the traffic the spend bought. Consequence: spend is about 34.2x revenue (1,085,259,569 local vs 31,756,120.64). | Never rescaled. Media efficiency (CTR, CPC, CPM) and funnel conversion are reported as measured; commercial return leads with the scale-free Revenue-to-Spend Index; absolute ROAS and CAC appear only under a data note; the Click-to-Lead % measure makes the gap visible. |
| ANOMALY | Organic, owned and referral channels carry 'ad spend' | 3 non-paid channels hold 435,199,001 of local spend. | Kept and labelled 'channel cost'; ROAS on these channels carries a caveat. |
| ANOMALY | Media rates are the same on every channel | CTR runs 4.21%-4.25% on all 7 channels - Display included, where 0.1-0.5% is typical - and owned, organic and referral channels record impressions and clicks as if bought. | Media rates are reported for paid media in total and never ranked by channel: there is no signal to rank. |
| ANOMALY | Activity before the campaign StartDate | 33,739 spend rows (42% of spend) predate their campaign's StartDate, median 238 days early. | StartDate is recorded but never used as a filter; flagged IsBeforeCampaignStart. |
| EXCEPTION | Same-day touches | 12,424 touches share a date with another touch of the same lead. | Ties broken by the source TouchSequence; time-decay gives same-day touches equal weight. |
| EXCEPTION | The newest lead cohorts are still converting | Lead to booked revenue takes up to 58 days. Leads created in August 2026 show 2.1% conversion by the as-of date against 13.5% once every recorded outcome is in. | Conversion trends leave out cohorts younger than the conversion window (Lead to Customer % (Mature Cohorts)); totals are stated 'by the as-of date'. |
| EXCEPTION | Several spend lines share Date x Campaign x Channel | 9,275 groups hold 2+ lines; 0 are exact duplicates and 7,133 mix currencies. | Treated as separate billing lines and summed after conversion. Not deduplicated. |
| EXCEPTION | Messy campaign aliases | 900 raw names resolve to 300 campaigns, 3 aliases each, 0 ambiguous. Styles differ in case and separators, and 61 aliases spell the ANZ region 'AU-NZ'. Example: ['anz-brand-002', 'ANZ Brand 002', 'AU-NZ-Brand-002']. | Power Query rules in CampaignAliasResolution: R1 ignore case, R2 space/hyphen/underscore are one separator, R3 collapse separator runs, R4 AU-NZ = ANZ. Every alias checked against the supplied mapping. |
| EXCEPTION | Lead country is independent of campaign region | Within each country the most common campaign region covers only 21%-22% of leads. | Row-level security filters by campaign Region: a regional team sees the credit its own campaigns earned. |
| INFO | Keys and completeness are clean | 0 duplicate primary keys, 0 null cells, 0 orphaned foreign keys across 7 relationships. | Foreign keys declared and engine-trusted in dbo. |
| INFO | Journeys are complete and pre-conversion | Every lead has 1-6 touches; 40,152 (80%) span 2+ channels; 0 touches fall after lead creation. | The conversion event for attribution is lead creation. |
| INFO | Segments do not differ in deal size or conversion | Average deal Enterprise $4,748, Mid-Market $4,854, SMB $4,922; lead-to-customer 13.0%-13.3%. An Enterprise deal is no larger than an SMB deal, unlike real B2B data. | Segment stays available as a slicer and heatmap axis. No segment-value story is told, and the heatmap colours only statistically significant differences. |
