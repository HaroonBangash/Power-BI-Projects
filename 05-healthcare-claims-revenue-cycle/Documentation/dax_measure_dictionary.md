# DAX Measure Dictionary - Healthcare Claims & Revenue Cycle

Generated by `Python/03_generate_measure_dictionary.py` from `Python/model_measures.py`. **Value (all data)** is each measure at the grand total - nothing selected - measured in the live Power BI model by `Validation/reconcile_measures.ps1`, not typed by hand.

Reading the values: with no date selected, a STOCK (open AR, AR claims, AR age) is read at the as-of month end, 30 Nov 2026, and a FLOW (claims, billed, allowed, collected, denials, AR movement) covers the whole period, Jan 2023 to Nov 2026. Amounts are **AUD** - every facility state is Australian and the payers are Medicare, Bupa, Medibank, HCF and NIB. Two figures rest on a stated ASSUMPTION held in ModelConfig - the timely-filing window, and that patient responsibility leaves the payer receivable on the payer's payment date - and both say so. The data is synthetic.

**118 measures in 11 folders, plus 2 calculation groups (10 items).**

## Calculation groups

A calculation group applies to the measure a visual already shows, so a comparison is one selection rather than six copies of every measure.

### Date Basis (precedence 10)

Which clock the calendar is measuring. A claim has four dates - when the care happened, when the claim was submitted, when the payer adjudicated it, and when it resolved into cash or a denial - and they answer different questions. Service date is the active relationship; the other three are switched on here with USERELATIONSHIP, so one fact table answers all four questions instead of four copies of it. Lower precedence than Time Comparison, so a comparison is applied to whichever basis is selected rather than the other way round.

| Item | Does |
|---|---|
| Service date | When the care was delivered. The default, and the right basis for clinical volume. |
| Submission date | When the claim reached the payer. The right basis for anything about the receivable, because this is when the payer's clock starts. |
| Adjudication date | When the payer decided. The right basis for denial workload, because that is the day the work lands on somebody's desk. |
| Resolution date | When the claim turned into cash or a denial. The right basis for a cash question. Pending claims have no resolution date and correctly fall out. |

### Time Comparison (precedence 20)

Applies a time comparison to whatever measure a visual shows. Higher precedence than Date Basis, so the comparison wraps the basis: 'prior month on submission date' means the month before, measured on submission. A comparison is BLANK where there is nothing to compare against, rather than reporting the whole of a figure as growth.

| Item | Does |
|---|---|
| Selected period | The measure as it stands, with no comparison applied. |
| Prior month | The same measure one month earlier. Blank in the first month the data covers. |
| Month over month | This month less last month. |
| Month over month % | The change as a share of last month. Blank when last month is blank or zero, because a percentage of nothing is not infinity - it is unanswerable. |
| Prior year | The same measure twelve months earlier. Blank where the data does not reach back that far, rather than comparing against nothing. |
| Trailing 12 months | The measure summed over the twelve months ending in the current one. A stock measure is unaffected by this, which is correct: a receivable has no twelve-month total. |

| Measure | Value (all data) | Description |

|---|---|---|

| Measure | Value (all data) | Description |

| AR Claims | 23,065 | Claims sitting in AR at the snapshot month end. |
