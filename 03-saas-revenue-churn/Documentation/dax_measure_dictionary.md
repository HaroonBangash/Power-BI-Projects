# DAX Measure Dictionary - SaaS Revenue, Retention & Churn

Generated by `Python/03_generate_measure_dictionary.py` from `Python/model_measures.py`. **Value (all data)** is each measure at the grand total - nothing selected - measured in the live Power BI model by `Validation/reconcile_measures.ps1`, not typed by hand.

Reading the values: with no date selected, a STOCK (MRR, ARR, customers, seats) is read at the as-of date, 31 Aug 2026, and a FLOW (new business, churn, invoices, tickets) covers the whole period, Jan 2022 to Aug 2026. Amounts are USD. Two measures - lifetime value and CAC payback - depend on a gross-margin ASSUMPTION held in ModelConfig, and say so. The data is synthetic.

**94 measures in 13 folders, plus 1 calculation groups (6 items).**

## Calculation groups

A calculation group applies to the measure a visual already shows, so a comparison is one selection rather than six copies of every measure.

### Time Comparison (precedence 10)

Applies a time comparison to whatever measure a visual shows: the period itself, the month before, the change, or the same month a year earlier. A comparison is BLANK where the model has no data to compare against, rather than showing the whole of a figure as growth.

| Item | Does |
|---|---|
| Selected period | The measure as it stands, with no comparison applied. |
| Prior month | The same measure one month earlier. Blank in the first month the data covers. |
| Month over month | This month less last month. |
| Month over month % | The change as a share of last month. Blank when last month is blank or zero, because a percentage of nothing is not infinity - it is unanswerable. |
| Prior year | The same measure twelve months earlier. Blank where the data does not reach back that far. |
| Year over year % | Growth on the same month a year earlier, with the same guard: a year the ledger does not cover is blank, not infinite growth. |

## 04 Retention & Churn

| Measure | Value (all data) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Logo Churn % | (blank) | Customers lost in the period as a share of the customers it opened with. The logo view: every customer counts the same whatever they pay. A month in which nobody left reads 0%, not blank - DIVIDE ( BLANK, n ) is BLANK, which would leave a GAP in the line where the best month should be. A month the data does not reach is BLANK, not 0% - otherwise a chart ends on a perfect month that is only an absence of data. |
| Opening Customers | (blank) | Customers on the books at the end of the month BEFORE the period in context. |
| Revenue Churn % | (blank) | MRR lost in the period as a share of opening MRR. The revenue view: a departing Enterprise customer weighs twenty times a departing Starter, which is why this sits beside logo churn and never replaces it. A month the data does not reach is BLANK, not 0% - otherwise a chart ends on a perfect month that is only an absence of data. |
| Logo Retention % | (blank) | The complement of logo churn: the share of opening customers still here at the end of the period. |
| Gross Revenue Retention % | (blank) | Opening MRR less churn and contraction, over opening MRR. Expansion is deliberately excluded: GRR asks what the book keeps without selling anything more, so it can never exceed 100%. |
| Net Revenue Retention % | (blank) | Gross revenue retention plus expansion and reactivation. In THIS source there is neither, so NRR and GRR are identical by construction - both are published so the reader can see that, rather than being shown the flattering one alone. Any NRR above 100% on this data would be an arithmetic error. |
| NRR less GRR (pp) | (blank) | The gap between net and gross revenue retention, in percentage points - the contribution of expansion. It is zero here, and that zero is a finding about the data rather than a blank cell. |
| Annualised Logo Churn % | 14.10% | The trailing-twelve-month logo churn rate, so a monthly figure can be read against an annual retention target. Blank until twelve complete months exist. |
| Trailing 12m Revenue Retention % | 91.00% | Gross revenue retention measured over the twelve months ending at the as-of month. A fixed window, so it means the same thing whatever is selected on the page - which is why it is a card measure and not a chart one. |
| Logo Retention % (Latest Month) | (blank) | Logo retention in the newest complete month, whatever period is selected elsewhere. A card measure: a retention rate needs a window, and over all time there is no month before the first one to open with. |
| Gross Revenue Retention % (Latest Month) | (blank) | Gross revenue retention in the newest complete month, on the same fixed window as its logo counterpart. |
| Net Revenue Retention % (Latest Month) | (blank) | Net revenue retention in the newest complete month. Equal to the gross figure beside it, because this source has no expansion - which is the point of showing both. |
| NRR less GRR (Latest Month, pp) | (blank) | The gap between net and gross revenue retention in the newest complete month, in percentage points. It reads 0.00, and that zero is a finding about the data rather than an empty card. |
