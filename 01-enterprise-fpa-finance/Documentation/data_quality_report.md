# Data Quality Report - Enterprise FP&A Finance

Generated by `Python/01_data_audit.py` from the raw CSVs in `Data/raw` (never from SQL). The data is **synthetic**; nothing here describes a real company.

**5 ERROR, 10 ANOMALY, 5 EXCEPTION, 4 INFO.** As-of date: **2026-08-31** (the last ledger day).

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ERROR | Breaks correctness unless the build handles it |
| ANOMALY | Implausible for real data and not repairable: reported, never hidden or rescaled |
| EXCEPTION | Legitimate, but needs an explicit modelling rule |
| INFO | Confirmed good, or descriptive |

## 1. [ERROR] Calendar ends before the last sub-ledger date

dim_date ends 2026-08-31, but AR due dates run to 2026-10-30 and payments to 2027-01-10 (AP: 2026-10-15 / 2026-11-22). 1,902 due or paid dates have no calendar day.

**Handling:** DimDate regenerated to 2027-06-30 (end of FY27) and checked against the supplied calendar for all 1,704 overlapping days.

## 2. [ERROR] Payments recorded after the as-of date

The ledger, budget, cash and FX all end 2026-08-31, yet 769 AR receipts (7,382,038.79 AUD) and 395 AP payments (2,328,737.98) are dated later. Their Status says Paid, but on the as-of date they were open.

**Handling:** As-of date = 2026-08-31, the last ledger day. Every balance is computed AS OF a date: a document is open on day D if issued by D and not paid by D. Status is never read for balances.

## 3. [ERROR] The AUD-to-AUD rate is not 1

AUD is the reporting currency, yet 1,697 of 1,704 daily AUD rates differ from 1 (0.9504 to 1.0475). Applying them would move the AUD entity's figures by up to 5% for no reason.

**Handling:** AUD is fixed at exactly 1.000000 in the build; the supplied value is kept in an audit column.

## 4. [ERROR] Actuals are in six local currencies; everything else is in AUD

Each entity books in its own currency (80,000 of 80,000 lines match the entity's currency), while budget, forecast, AR, AP and cash are in AUD. Revenue is stored as credits (negative) and costs as debits, so a raw sum mixes currencies and signs.

**Handling:** Every GL line is translated to AUD in SQL (FactGL.AmountAUD). Amounts keep the ledger sign; measures present revenue and costs as positive numbers and profit as revenue less costs.

## 5. [ERROR] The forecast 'Scenario' column is not a scenario

Every one of the 60,480 forecast lines carries exactly one label - Base 36,315, Best 12,006, Worst 12,159 - so filtering to 'Base' keeps 60% of the plan and drops the rest. The label does not change the value: forecast/budget on revenue lines is 1.001 for Worst and 1.000 for Best.

**Handling:** The forecast is one version (all 60,480 lines). The label is kept as a line attribute and reported here. Scenarios are modelled properly with disconnected driver tables (revenue, cost and FX assumptions) - see the scenario page.

## 6. [ANOMALY] Daily exchange rates are noise around a flat mean

Rates move 1.66-1.72% a day on average - several times a real G10 currency - yet each currency's annual mean stays within 0.30% across five years. Converting each line at its own day's rate adds random noise that is not an economic effect.

**Handling:** P&L actuals are translated at the MONTHLY AVERAGE rate (mean of the month's daily rates, 6 dp) - the IAS 21 average-rate practice. The daily spot translation is kept per line for audit; the totals differ by less than 0.01%.

## 7. [ANOMALY] The ledger is a partial extract of the plan's scope

Payroll (6000 Salaries) posts in only 2,292 of 3,360 department-months (68.2%); 19,468 of 60,480 budget lines (32.2%) have no posting at all. A real ledger posts payroll to every cost centre every month.

**Handling:** Reported, never filled in or rescaled. Variance to budget is shown as measured and labelled; the report leads with measures that do not depend on coverage (margins, mix, trends within the ledger).

## 8. [ANOMALY] Actuals sit at a fixed fraction of budget in every month

Revenue runs at 69% of budget (range 51%-84%), operating expense at 67% and COGS at 117% - in each of the 56 months, with no trend. The forecast tracks the budget (monthly totals 96.8%-102.7% of it), not the actuals.

**Handling:** The gap is structural, so it is presented as a plan-calibration finding (a real FP&A team would re-base the plan), not as 56 months of performance news. Variance commentary focuses on what moves: changes against the usual gap.

## 9. [ANOMALY] Local amounts share one scale in every currency

The median revenue line is 3,593-3,725 local units in all six currencies, and every entity's budget is about the same in AUD. After translation an entity's size therefore follows its exchange rate: actual revenue is 96% of budget in the UK (GBP 1.92) but 54% in Australia (AUD 1.00).

**Handling:** Translation is still applied - it is the correct treatment of the data as labelled. Entities are compared on currency-neutral ratios (gross margin, cost-to-revenue), which translation cannot distort; entity variance to budget carries this note.

## 10. [ANOMALY] The group runs at an operating loss - in the plan as well as the ledger

FY26: revenue 17,323,588 AUD against operating expense 22,398,340; operating margin -86%. The budget itself plans -67%. Revenue is booked to every department, HR and Legal included.

**Handling:** Reported as measured. Revenue is analysed by entity and stream; departments are treated as cost centres.

## 11. [ANOMALY] Budget lines jump from month to month

One budget line (entity x department x account) varies by a median 57% of its mean between months, with no seasonality; totals are stable. Month-level variance on a single line is therefore mostly noise in the plan.

**Handling:** Variance is analysed at levels where the plan is stable (account group, entity, department, quarter), and a line is called out only when its variance departs from its own usual range.

## 12. [ANOMALY] Accruals are never reversed

About a quarter of lines are 'Accrued' in every year (2022: 25%, 2023: 24%, 2024: 25%, 2025: 25%, 2026: 25%). Accruals are normally replaced by the actual invoice within weeks.

**Handling:** Both statuses count as actuals (accrual accounting). The accrued share is shown as a close-quality indicator.

## 13. [ANOMALY] A fixed share of receivables is never collected - and never written off

18.3% of invoiced value has no receipt, and the share is the same for 2023 invoices as for 2026 ones (unpaid invoices by issue year: 2023: 880, 2024: 861, 2025: 852, 2026: 581; 2026 is eight months). On 2026-08-31, 22,998,909 AUD of the 40,197,880 open is more than a year past due. AP shows the same pattern (12.2% unpaid).

**Handling:** Reported as measured, with ageing as of any date. Days sales outstanding on 2026-08-31 is 314 (90-day count-back) against 74 days to collect a paid invoice - the gap is the uncollected balance, shown separately so it is not mistaken for slow payment.

## 14. [ANOMALY] Receivables do not reconcile to ledger revenue

AR invoiced 49,767,107 AUD in calendar 2025 against 17,332,091 of GL revenue (2.87x). The sub-ledgers and the GL are independent extracts.

**Handling:** Working-capital days use each sub-ledger's own flows (AR invoicing for DSO, AP bills for DPO), never GL revenue.

## 15. [ANOMALY] Cash balances are a random walk with a floor

Each entity's closing cash moves 51,444 AUD a day on average in either direction, unrelated to ledger, receipts or payments; Northstar Holdings sits at exactly 50,000.00 on 33 days (2023-06-28 to 2025-03-23) - a generator floor.

**Handling:** Balances reported as supplied (closing balance = last day of the period, never summed over time). No cash-flow bridge is drawn: the sources cannot support one.

## 16. [EXCEPTION] Interest, FX gain/loss and tax are not budgeted

Accounts 7000, 7100, 7200 have actuals but no budget or forecast line.

**Handling:** Plan comparisons are like-for-like down to OPERATING PROFIT. Below-the-line items are shown as actuals only - never as a 100% variance.

## 17. [EXCEPTION] Balance-sheet accounts have no ledger postings

Accounts 1000, 1100, 1200, 2000, 2100 (cash, receivables, inventory, payables, accruals) exist in the chart but never in the GL.

**Handling:** The ledger is a P&L extract. Cash comes from the daily balances, receivables and payables from the AR and AP sub-ledgers. Inventory has no source, so days inventory outstanding is not reported.

## 18. [EXCEPTION] FX gain/loss carries both signs

1,658 FX lines are gains (credits) and 2,173 are losses.

**Handling:** Legitimate: kept signed, reported as a net below-the-line item.

## 19. [EXCEPTION] Customer and vendor are optional on ledger lines

10,605 lines carry a customer (revenue accounts only) and 37,856 a vendor (cost accounts only); the rest carry neither.

**Handling:** Nullable foreign keys; blank is shown as 'Not specified'.

## 20. [EXCEPTION] Security scopes two dimensions, but three facts have only one

18 users are scoped by entity and department ('ALL' = no restriction). AR, AP and cash carry an entity but no department.

**Handling:** Entity scope applies everywhere. A department-scoped user (e.g. the Sales manager) sees their department's P&L across entities, and NO receivables, payables or cash - company-wide treasury data is not part of a cost-centre view.

## 21. [INFO] Keys and completeness are clean

0 duplicate keys across 14 files, 0 nulls in required columns, 0 orphaned references across 11 relationships. Nulls occur only where they carry meaning: an unpaid invoice has no PaidDate; a GL line has a customer or a vendor or neither.

**Handling:** Foreign keys declared and engine-trusted in dbo.

## 22. [INFO] Supplied calendar is correct; financial year runs July to June

1,704 contiguous days 2022-01-01 to 2026-08-31; 0 days disagree with an independent recomputation of all nine columns. FY26 = 1 Jul 2025 - 30 Jun 2026 (the Australian year, consistent with an AUD group).

**Handling:** Regenerated days are checked against the supplied calendar for every overlapping day.

## 23. [INFO] AR statuses are internally consistent

18,000 of 18,000 documents: Paid always has a paid date and amount, Open never; 0 partial payments. Payment terms: 14, 30, 45, 60 days.

**Handling:** No cleansing needed; balances are still derived from dates (see the as-of rule).

## 24. [INFO] AP statuses are internally consistent

12,000 of 12,000 documents: Paid always has a paid date and amount, Open never; 0 partial payments. Payment terms: 14, 30, 45 days.

**Handling:** No cleansing needed; balances are still derived from dates (see the as-of rule).
