# DAX Measure Dictionary - Enterprise FP&A Finance

Generated by `Python/03_generate_measure_dictionary.py` from `Python/model_measures.py`. **Value (all data)** is each measure at the grand total - no year, entity or department selected - measured in the live Power BI model by `Validation/reconcile_measures.ps1`, not typed by hand.

Reading the values: with no version selected a measure means the ACTUALS; with no date selected a balance means the as-of date (31 Aug 2026) and a flow means the whole period (Jan 2022 to Aug 2026). Money is AUD, the ledger translated at monthly average rates. The data is synthetic.

**133 measures in 11 folders, plus 2 calculation groups (11 items).**

## Calculation groups

A calculation group applies to the measure a visual already shows, so Actual, Budget, Forecast and the variances are one selection rather than four copies of every measure.

### Plan Version (precedence 20)

Actual, Budget and Forecast, and the variance between them, as one selection applied to every income-statement measure. Variance is favourable-positive: for a cost, an underspend is positive. Where the plan has no line - interest, FX, tax - the variance is blank, never 100%.

| Item | Does |
|---|---|
| Actual | General ledger, translated to AUD at monthly average rates. |
| Budget | The annual budget. Covers revenue, COGS and operating expense only. |
| Forecast | The forecast, as one version of all its lines. |
| Var vs Budget | Actual less budget, signed so that positive is favourable. Margin lines are shown in percentage points. |
| Var % vs Budget | Favourable variance as a share of the budget. Blank on margin lines, where a percentage of a percentage would mislead. |
| Var vs Forecast | Actual less forecast, signed so that positive is favourable. |
| Var % vs Forecast | Favourable variance as a share of the forecast. |

### Period View (precedence 10)

How much of time a figure covers: the period in view, the financial year to date, the trailing twelve months, or the same window a year earlier. Every window ends at the balance date - the last day in context capped at the as-of date - so a part year is never compared with a full one.

| Item | Does |
|---|---|
| Selected period | The period the page's filters select. |
| Year to date | From 1 July to the end of the period in view, never past the as-of date. |
| Prior year | The same window one year earlier. The window is capped at the as-of date BEFORE it is shifted, so a part year is compared with the same part year; blank where the ledger does not cover that earlier window. |
| Prior year to date | 1 July to the same point of last year - the like-for-like comparison. |

## 01 Model Controls

| Measure | Value (all data) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| As Of Date | 08/31/2026 00:00:00 | Last day of the general ledger, read from ModelConfig. Every figure, balance and ageing is stated as of this date. Never TODAY(). |
| Reporting Currency | AUD | Group reporting currency (AUD). The ledger is translated at monthly average rates; everything else is supplied in it. |
| Balance Date | 08/31/2026 00:00:00 | The date every balance is stated on: the last day in the current filter context, capped at the as-of date. With FY26 selected it is 30 Jun 2026; with no date filter, the as-of date. BLANK for a period that starts after the as-of date, so a balance never runs flat into months that have no data. |
| Months Remaining in FY | 10 | Whole months between the as-of date and the end of the financial year it falls in: the months the outlook has to project. |
| Report Context | As of 31 Aug 2026   |   FY22 to FY27   |   AUD at monthly average rates   |   Synthetic data | The one-line context every page carries: as-of date, period in view, currency and basis. |
| Selected Financial Year | All financial years | Financial year or years in the current filter context, for page titles. |

## 02 Income Statement

| Measure | Value (all data) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| PL Ledger Amount *(hidden)* | $86,847,393 | Ledger-sign amount (debit +, credit -) for the version in context. With no version filter it is the ACTUAL: versions are never summed. Every income-statement measure builds on this one. |
| Is Actual Version *(hidden)* | True | TRUE when the context is the actuals - no version filter, or exactly ACT. Below-the-line measures use it to stay blank under Budget and Forecast, which do not carry those accounts. |
| Revenue | $80,234,572 | Revenue accounts (4000-4020), presented positive. Credits in the ledger, so the ledger sum is negated. |
| COGS | $44,794,575 | Cost of goods sold (5000-5100), presented positive. |
| Gross Profit | $35,439,997 | Revenue less cost of goods sold. |
| Gross Margin % | 44.17% | Gross profit as a share of revenue. Currency-neutral: translation moves revenue and COGS by the same rate, so entities can be compared on it (PROJECT_STATE D12). |
| Operating Expense | $103,819,546 | Operating expense accounts (6000-6110), presented positive. |
| Operating Profit | $-68,379,548 | Gross profit less operating expense: the last line the budget covers, and so the last line with a variance. |
| Operating Margin % | -85.22% | Operating profit as a share of revenue. |
| Other Expense (Net) | $9,991,808 | Interest and FX gain or loss, net (7000, 7100). Actuals only - unbudgeted, so never shown as a variance. |
| Tax Expense | $8,476,037 | Tax expense (7200). Actuals only - unbudgeted. |
| Net Profit | $-86,847,393 | Operating profit less other expense and tax. Actuals only: a budget net profit would silently equal the budget operating profit, because the plan has no below-the-line lines. |
| Net Margin % | -108.24% | Net profit as a share of revenue. |
| COGS % of Revenue | 55.83% | Cost of goods sold as a share of revenue; currency-neutral. |
| Opex % of Revenue | 129.40% | Operating expense as a share of revenue; currency-neutral, so it compares entities without the exchange-rate level distorting them. |
| P&L Line Value | (blank) | The value of the income-statement line in context (DimPLLine): groups, subtotals and margins in one measure, so the statement reads as a statement. Ratio lines are formatted as percentages by the calculation group. |
| P&L Bridge Amount | (blank) | The statement line as a CONTRIBUTION to profit: revenue positive, every cost negative, so a waterfall of the group lines nets exactly to net profit. Use it only with DimPLLine filtered to the group lines. |
| Account Amount | $247,316,537 | Account-level amount in its natural sign: revenue positive because it is income, costs positive because they are costs. Use it for account detail; a grand total that mixes both is meaningless by construction. |
